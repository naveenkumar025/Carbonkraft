The problem

Green ambition is abundant. Verification is not.

Identity has Aadhaar. Payments have UPI. Emissions have a stack of paper utility bills and a buyer in Brussels who doesn't trust any of it.

MSMEs locked out of green finance

Without a verifiable emissions record, small manufacturers can't access concessional green credit — even when their actual footprint qualifies.

Compliance in a language no one on the floor speaks

EU-CBAM reporting formats assume a sustainability team that a 40-person export unit simply doesn't have.

Satellite data and shop-floor data never meet

Geospatial verification exists. Utility records exist. Almost nothing cross-checks them against each other automatically.

Regulation moves faster than paperwork

By the time a compliance filing is finalized, the underlying formula has often already changed upstream in Brussels.

The platform

One verification layer. Three sovereign rails.

CarbonKrafft isn't another dashboard. It's infrastructure: a data layer, a compute layer, and an interoperability layer designed to move together.

Liquidity

Consent-based utility data flow — an MSME's records move once, verifiably, instead of being re-keyed for every auditor.

Sovereignty

Bhashini NLP reads the paperwork in its native language; ISRO and Sentinel geospatial assets confirm it from orbit.

Interoperability

Plugs directly into National Project Management Agency dashboards — a result should be usable the moment it's produced.
